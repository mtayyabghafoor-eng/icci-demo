ICCI Control Dashboard

A working prototype for the Islamic Cultural Centre of Ireland, Clonskeagh
Stage 1 for review: Inventory and assets
Everything you see is made up. Every name, room, item and number in these screens is fictional test data, marked "Test". No real person or record of the Centre is in the system.

What this is

One secure website, used only by two or three Centre admins, to see what the Centre owns, where it is, and what has happened to it. It works on a computer and on a phone. Every change is recorded with who did it and when, and nothing is ever deleted.

What we are asking you

Please look at each workflow below and tell us whether it matches how the Centre actually works. Each section ends with a few short questions. A one-page feedback sheet is at the end. Honest "this would not work here" answers are the most useful kind.

Workflow 1

Signing In Safely

Each admin signs in with their email, a password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on their phone. After 15 minutes without use, the system signs them out, with a two-minute warning first.

Please check
  • Is a code from a phone app acceptable for every admin?
  • Is 15 minutes the right time before an automatic sign-out?
Workflow 2

The Home Page

The first screen shows what needs attention today: shelves that are low or empty, items that are missing or damaged, and entries waiting for an admin's approval. Each box opens its list.

Please check
  • Are these the things you would want to see first each morning?
Workflow 3

Finding Any Item

Everything the Centre owns or keeps in stock is in one list. You can search by name or label number, and filter by kind, category, location and condition.

Please check
  • Are these the right details to keep for each item (value, purchase date, warranty, photo, notes)?
  • Are the categories and the building, room, store room and shelf layout right for the Centre?
Workflow 4

Labels and Registering a New Item

Every item gets a printed sticker with a QR code and a number such as ICCI-000123. Labels are printed in sheets before they are used. Scanning a new label with a phone starts registering the item; scanning a used label opens the item. The code holds only the number, nothing private.

  1. Print a sheet of new labels.
  2. Stick a label on the item, or on the shelf for things like soap and bin bags.
  3. Scan it and fill in two short steps: what it is, then where it is.
Please check
  • Would one sticker per piece of furniture or equipment be practical?
  • For shelf stock (cleaning supplies, tissues and so on), is one sticker per shelf right?
Workflow 5

Stock: Counts, Deliveries and Corrections

For things that get used up, the system never just overwrites a number. It keeps every count and every delivery, and works out the current amount from them. A mistake is fixed with a correction and a reason, and a different admin must approve it. Nobody can approve their own entry.

Please check
  • Is a weekly count realistic? Who would do it, and which store rooms?
  • Are you comfortable that every correction needs a second admin to approve it?
Workflow 6

Furniture and Equipment

Fixed assets are single things such as chairs, kettles, projectors and carpets. From the item's page an admin can move it, report damage, mark it missing, or ask for it to be disposed of. Each one records a note, an optional photo, and who reported it. Disposal needs a second admin's approval.

Please check
  • Should any other action be recorded, for example lending an item to another organisation?
  • Is a second approval for disposal right, or too strict for low-value items?
Workflow 7

Stocktake of a Room

An admin walks around one room with a phone and scans every label. At the end the system lists what was expected but not found, and anything found in the wrong place. Nothing changes until a different admin approves each difference.

  1. Pick the room and start the stocktake.
  2. Scan each label: tap "Seen here" for furniture, or enter the number on a shelf.
  3. Check the list of what was not found, then send it for approval.
  4. Another admin approves or rejects each difference.
Please check
  • Which rooms would you stocktake first, and how often?
Workflow 8

Lists and the Asset Register

Workflow 9

Who Can See What

Each admin has a role. Centre admins use every part of the system. A finance account sees only what finance needs; in this stage that is just the home page. The database itself enforces these rules, not only the screens, and this is tested for every role and for anyone not signed in.

Please check
  • Who would hold each role at the Centre? (No names needed yet, only the number of people.)
What comes after

The Road from Prototype to Daily Use

The system is built in stages, so each part is checked by you before the next is added.

StageWhat it coversStatus
1Inventory and assets (this pack)Ready for your review
2People directory, Garda vetting status and dates, training dates, policies and sign-offs, vehicles, and a renewals register with email remindersIn progress
3Weekly rota (prayers, Jumu'ah, security, cleaning, reception, Ramadan and Eid templates) and a maintenance logPlanned
4Lending libraryPlanned
5Expenses with approvals and fund tags, and a one-click monthly Board reportPlanned
6Service registers (funerals, shahada, nikah), once those services have settledLater

Steps before real use

  1. Your feedback on this stage. We adjust the workflows to fit how the Centre really works.
  2. Stage 2 review pack. The same kind of walkthrough for people, vetting and training.
  3. A written proposal. It covers the running costs (secure hosting in the EU, backups), the build fee, ongoing support, and what is included.
  4. Agreement and data protection. A short written agreement, including how the Centre's data is handled and how long records of people who leave are kept.
  5. A small pilot. Real data in one area, for example one store room and its shelves, for a few weeks.
  6. Training and go-live. A short session for each admin, then the rest of the Centre is added room by room.

Already built in

  • All data is kept in the European Union.
  • Two-step sign-in and automatic sign-out.
  • Nothing is ever deleted; every change is recorded with who and when.
  • Nobody can approve their own entry.
  • Garda vetting: only the status and dates, never the disclosure itself.
