# Design and ease-of-use review, 27 November 2026

Written after three outside reviews by Gemini (Carbon, Neon, Argon) were
checked against the code and against the live screens. This is a plan,
not a decision. Nothing in the app was changed. It follows `CLAUDE.md`,
`docs/PLAN.md` and `docs/UI-PLAN.md`; nothing here adds a dependency or a
database change unless a question below says so.

## 1. How this was checked

- 31 screens opened on a PC (1280 pixels wide) and a phone (Pixel 7 size),
  plus Home and two detail pages on a tablet (iPad size), as the fictional
  Test Super Admin on the local test database. Nothing was saved or changed.
- Every page was checked automatically for: sideways scrolling on a phone,
  boxes without a label, buttons without a name, text under 12 pixels, and
  things to tap that are smaller than a fingertip (44 pixels).
- Each Gemini claim was looked up in the code.
- The design checklist the owner supplied (the "UI/UX Pro Max" skill) was
  used by hand, in its order: access for everyone, touch, layout, type,
  forms, finding your way. Its search tool is not installed on this
  computer, so no answer here comes from its database.

Evidence pictures: `docs/screens/review-ui-*.png`.

**The local test database is full of leftovers from test runs** (940
switched-off places, tickets named "Test run 2026-11-26…"). That is not a
design fault, but some lists look busier than they will in real use.

## 2. What is already good (keep it)

- On a phone, no page scrolls sideways (all 31 checked).
- Every box has a label a screen reader can read; every button has a name.
- Colours, status words beside colours, the gold focus ring, the
  "Skip to the page" link and the motion rules all follow UI-PLAN. Colour
  pairs are proved by the project's own contrast test.
- Plain words on buttons ("Count this shelf", "Report a problem").
- The New claim form (Phase 5) is the best-designed screen in the app:
  numbered parts, camera and gallery side by side, clear choices for how it
  was paid. It is the model for the fixes below.

A new colour scheme or font is not needed. The pasted skill can generate a
"design system"; this app already has one the owner approved (15 Nov 2026),
and replacing it would undo agreed work.

## 4. What all three reviews missed

1. **The tablet menu has no words.** From 768 to 1023 pixels the sidebar
   shows about 25 icons only (`review-ui-home-tablet.png`). The words appear
   only when a mouse hovers, and a touch screen has no hover. UI-PLAN 4.2
   promised a button to open it out; it was never built. This is the
   biggest gap in access found.
2. **Home cries wolf.** 9 of 12 lines are red. The three approval queues are
   counted twice (the gold "For you to approve" card and three red lines),
   and approvals are jobs, not alarms. The top cards repeat Low stock and
   Missing from the list below (`review-ui-home-desktop.png`).
3. **"No photo yet" is a dead end.** A large empty box on every item without
   a photo, with no button to add one (PC and phone).
4. **Filter boxes cut off their own words** on a PC: "Any conditic", "All
   categorie", "Anyone or nobo" (`review-ui-items-desktop.png`,
   `review-ui-tickets-filters-desktop.png`).
5. **Rota week: empty slots look like filled ones.** Same box, brown text.
   On a phone the week is one long scroll (about 15 screens) with no way to
   jump to today.
6. **Rota slot: 26 identical solid green buttons** ("Put on this slot"), so
   nothing stands out (`review-ui-rota-slot-desktop.png`).
7. **Weekly count opens on a nearly empty page** with one drop-down
   (`review-ui-weekly-count-phone.png`). A list of store rooms showing
   "last counted 6 days ago" would show at a glance which room is due.
8. **Units are not made plural:** "13 roll", "4 roll used".
9. **Small text:** menu group headings are 11 pixels; UI-PLAN says nothing
   under 14 except printed labels. "Changed" and "Today" on the rota too.
10. **Small things to tap on a phone:** policy-grid column titles (15 pixels
    tall), "Show switched-off places" (18), Back and the desk tabs (40, just
    under 44).
11. **The item page lists "Not recorded" six times.** Empty details could
    fold into one line: "Not recorded yet: warranty, make and model… Add".
12. **Form mistakes are shown as one message above Save;** the box at fault
    is not outlined in red and the cursor does not move to it. The shared
    form part already supports this; one form uses it.
13. **The main button sits in different places:** top right on Items and
    Places, under the intro on People and Policies, beside the count on
    Tickets.
14. **People list shows "Active" on every row** while it is showing active
    people only; long place paths wrap to three lines in the Items list.

## 5. Proposed Round D: clearer, calmer, quicker

Six slices, one per session, in this order. Each ends the usual way: tests,
full suite, screenshots in `docs/screens/`, one commit. No new dependency.
No database change except, if the owner says yes, copying last week's rota.

- **D1. Calmer screens.** Home (no double counting, plain badge words,
  approvals amber and only in their card, quick actions above the list on a
  phone); item page (one main button, red outline for Mark missing and
  Dispose, two-column details, empty details folded, "Add a photo" in the
  photo box); main button top right on every list; one label box on Items
  (PC); filter boxes wide enough for their words; plural units; no text
  under 13 pixels; no Sign out in the phone top bar.
- **D2. Tablet and touch.** Menu words on a tablet with an open-out button;
  every phone tap target at least 44 pixels; form mistakes outline the box
  and move the cursor to it.
- **D3. Photos and contacts.** Take a photo / Choose from photos / Remove
  photo on items, damage reports and tickets; tap to call and email on
  people pages; "in use" filter pills with × and Clear all.
- **D4. Counting.** Weekly count opens on a list of store rooms with when
  each was last counted, and shows "8 of 20 counted" in the Save bar.
  Stocktake shows found / expected, a "Still to find" list, and the camera
  reopens after each scan. No "Same" button.
- **D5. Rota.** Ready people first on the slot picker with a role filter,
  lighter "Put on" buttons, "Tick all empty days", "Go to a date", empty
  slots drawn as dashed boxes, day tabs on a phone; prayer times warn before
  leaving and keep the headings in view; copy last week's people if the
  owner says yes.
- **D6. People, policies, library, places.** Name filter on Record sign-off;
  Record vetting and training from list rows; library pages instead of a
  cut-off, +7 and +14 day buttons, camera on Lend and Return (Android);
  a search box on Places.

## 6. Questions for the owner

Replying "all recommended" is enough.

1. **Order.** (A) D1 to D6 in that order, one per session *(recommended:
   the first three are what everyone sees every day)*; (B) only D1 to D3
   now, the rest after trying them on the demo.
2. **Copy last week's people onto an empty rota week.** (A) Yes, as a
   button beside "Use a template", every vetting and double-booking warning
   still shown *(recommended: the same imam usually takes the same prayer)*;
   (B) no, templates only.
3. **Filters applying on their own.** (A) On a PC, the list updates a
   moment after a drop-down changes, and you stay where you were; on a phone
   the "Show" button stays *(recommended: safe for keyboard users and for
   folded phone filters)*; (B) keep the button everywhere, add only the
   "in use" pills.
4. **Home's top numbers.** (A) Keep four numbers but as a slim strip, and
   never count the same thing twice on Home *(recommended)*; (B) keep the
   big cards, fix only the badge words and the phone order.

**Owner's answers (27 November 2026): every recommended answer.** 1: (A)
D1 to D6 in order. 2: (A) yes, copy last week's people. 3: (A) PC filters
apply on their own, phones keep the button. 4: (A) slim strip, nothing
counted twice. Owner: "Screens need to be calmer… people look at the
design first." New request, slice **D7**: PDFs (policies first) shown as a
flip book like heyzine.com, English turning left to right and Arabic right
to left; each policy version holds an English and an Arabic PDF. Built
into the app with Mozilla's free PDF reader (`pdfjs-dist`, new add-on,
owner approved), so documents never leave ICCI's storage.
